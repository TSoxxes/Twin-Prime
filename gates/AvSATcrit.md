# Gate Specification — AvSAT\(_{\mathrm{crit}}\)

## Informal role

AvSAT\(_{\mathrm{crit}}\) is the averaged saturated large-shift smooth-excess gate. It is the remaining smooth-excess obstruction after small-shift R-FBDH-style inputs and slack reductions are applied.

## Schematic normalized form

\[
\left|
\sum_{\ell\sim L}\Gamma_\ell
\sum_{q_A\sim Q_A}\Alpha_{q_A}
\sum_{n\sim N^\ast}\Beta_n
\mathcal K^0(n;q_A,\ell)
\right|
\ll_A (\log X)^{-A},
\]

with saturated conductor condition

\[
Q_A L N^\ast\asymp X.
\]

## Status

Useful for the conditional manuscript. Not currently the highest-priority conjecture-facing route, because it remains close to standard large-sieve/Kloosterman barriers.
